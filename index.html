Project briefing

About CrisisSense

CrisisSense is an NLP-based system for classifying crisis-related language in text into three categories: no crisis, implicit crisis, and explicit crisis. It compares two independent approaches: TF-IDF + Logistic Regression and BERT.

Classification

The three classes

no_crisis

No Crisis

Text that does not indicate a crisis situation.

implicit_crisis

Implicit Crisis

Crisis-related meaning expressed indirectly rather than through a direct statement.

explicit_crisis

Explicit Crisis

Crisis-related content expressed directly.
